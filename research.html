Research

I am a mathematics Ph.D. student working with Marianna Csörnyei at the University of Chicago. My research is in geometric measure theory.

Currently, I am working on applying techniques from algorithmic information theory to problems in geometric measure theory, especially to establish sharp bounds on the Hausdorff dimension of radial projections.

During my undergraduate years, I worked in combinatorics. With Rosa Orellana, I spent a lot of time working on Stanley's Tree Isomorphism Conjecture. In parallel with my research in geometric measure theory, I am still working on this problem.
